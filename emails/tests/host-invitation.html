Hey, Accra.

Your next event deserves a guest list that doesn’t live in five different chats. BeCore Tickets is inviting Accra’s hosts and organisers onto a platform built for the work behind a good night.

Build a name they come back for. Get a public profile in our host directory, with your bio, social links and upcoming events together. Once verified, your Verified Host badge gives guests a visible trust signal. Ticket holders can follow you, so the connection doesn’t end when the music does.

Set the terms. Run RSVPs or paid ticket sales, set capacity and decide who needs approval. Manage your event from your own host dashboard.

Keep the door moving. Give your entry team check-in access, with QR tickets and duplicate-entry checks. The guest list can settle the argument.

Keep your crowd close. Share updates and conversations in The Room, the space for ticket holders, and manage VIP Concierge requests.

Know what worked. Track sign-ups, sales, arrivals and promoter links, with automatic reports to help plan your next event.

We consulted extensively with Kofi Bills, a key figure in Accra nightlife, during development. His insights helped shape the host and organiser tools around how events actually run.

For paid events, we integrate with Paystack and SeevPlus. Payments are verified with the provider before paid tickets are issued. BeCore Tickets doesn’t store card numbers or MoMo PINs.

Give us a reason to clear the calendar. Submit your event details; once approved, we’ll email a secure setup link for your dashboard.
