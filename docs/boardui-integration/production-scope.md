# Production scope

The production candidate excludes the four public evaluation pages at
`/boardui-trial`, `/boardui-trial/workspace`, `/boardui-trial/yorke-way` and
`/boardui-trial/yorke-way/signup`, their private styles/components, their isolated
launcher/check scripts and the trial-only pressable stylesheet.

The original experiment remains on `trial/boardui-tickets-20261101` at
`14b2b3789b61ed032e20ac772397c637c0fbfc33`. The complete supplied dashboard/customer
snapshot remains on `candidate/customer-dashboard-combined-20261102` at
`0e69c0e09eb729ef98f68b186964ba10b793bed2`. Historical screenshots and provenance
remain under `docs/boardui-trial` and `docs/boardui-integration`; their old trial
instructions apply to those snapshots, not this production candidate.

Real owner/organiser workspaces and customer routes retain their shared BoardUI
components, licence/provenance and `/boardui-pressable.css` adapter. No real
dashboard/customer route, access boundary or production data is removed.

This is source preparation only. Combined release admission requires separate
approval of exact source inventory and pins, followed by fresh hosted verification,
runtime proof and guarded deployment. Historical preview evidence is not proof of
the final combined release.
